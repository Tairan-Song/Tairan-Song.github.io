I am a Ph.D. student in computational science at Michigan State University, working in the MIDI Lab under Dr. Adam Alessio. My research focuses on feature extraction and representation learning from high-dimensional medical imaging data, particularly radiomics and graph-based methods for renal mass characterization from CT.

I am open to internship opportunities for summer 2027 in machine learning, medical imaging, and healthcare AI. Feel free to get in touch!
